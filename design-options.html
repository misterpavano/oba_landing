Trend Researcher
Luxury-meets-utility is a rising pattern. Think premium local brands. High differentiation from the "local consultant with a blue site" baseline.

Concern: warm dark is premium but may still read "tech/app" to a dentist who lands on it cold. Needs copy to bridge the warmth gap immediately. High upside if the copy lands.

Squad consensus: Option A fails the ICP test — beautiful but wrong audience. Option B converts best from cold SMB traffic, lowest risk. Option C has the best longevity — won't age, works across the widest range of business owners, clean to build on. Option D is the memorable bet — highest differentiation, highest upside if copy lands, slightly higher bounce risk from cold traffic.

If the goal is conversion: B or C. If the goal is standing out and being remembered: D. Hybrid play: C's layout and type system with D's dark color direction.
